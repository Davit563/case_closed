# The Missing Exam Papers

On Saturday afternoon, Ridgeway School held its open day for new families.

The sealed papers for Monday's final mathematics exam were locked in a filing cabinet in the Exams Office. Mr. Daniel Price checked them at **15:00**. At **17:28** he found the drawer unlocked and the papers gone.

## Door system

Every internal door at the school opens with a staff or prefect keycard. Each swipe is logged as an **entry** into that room. See `door_log.csv`.

## Rooms

Exams Office, Staff Room, Library, Science Lab, Gym, Main Hall

## Suspects

1. Mr. Daniel Price, head of exams
2. Ms. Rosa Delgado, chemistry teacher
3. Ethan Cole, a student and library prefect
4. Mr. Owen Fitch, the caretaker
5. Mrs. Julia Hart, deputy head

## Your task

Read the statements, the door log, and the clues. Exactly one suspect's story does not match the log. Who took the exam papers?
