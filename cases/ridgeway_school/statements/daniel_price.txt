Statement of Mr. Daniel Price

I checked the exam papers at 15:00. They were sealed and locked in the
cabinet. From about 15:10 I was in the Main Hall giving talks to visiting
families, and I didn't leave until about 17:25. Then I went back to the Exams
Office and found the drawer unlocked and the papers gone.

Only Julia and I have keys to that cabinet. She's been asking me all week
which topics come up on Monday. Her son Sam sits the paper.
