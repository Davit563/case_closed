Statement of Victor Crane

After dinner I stepped out to the Terrace for a cigarette. That was about
20:30, and I stayed out there until about 20:50, enjoying the air. Then I
went back to the Dining Room.

I never went near the Gallery after dinner. I'd already seen the painting
when Eleanor showed it to us.

Yes, I once offered to buy it. That was business, and it's long forgotten.
