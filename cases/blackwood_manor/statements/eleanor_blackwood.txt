Statement of Lady Eleanor Blackwood

I showed my guests The Heron at Dusk before dinner, and I looked in on it
again at about 20:00. It was hanging perfectly. I went back to the Dining
Room at about 20:10, then Julian and I went to the Library at about 20:15 to
look at some old letters. We stayed there until about 21:00, then went back
to the Dining Room together.

At about 21:30 I went to the Gallery to switch off the lamps and found the
frame empty.

I should mention that Victor Crane appraised the painting last spring and
offered to buy it. I refused. He didn't take it well.
