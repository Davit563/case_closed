# The Blackwood Heron

On Saturday evening, Lady Eleanor Blackwood held a small dinner party at Blackwood Manor.

*The Heron at Dusk*, a small oil painting worth about £400,000, hangs in the Gallery. Lady Eleanor showed it to her guests and checked on it at **20:02**. At **21:28** she found the frame empty.

## Door system

Every internal door at the manor opens with a personal keycard. Each swipe is logged as an **entry** into that room. See `door_log.csv`.

## Rooms

Gallery, Dining Room, Library, Study, Kitchen, Conservatory, Terrace

## Suspects

1. Lady Eleanor Blackwood, host and owner of the painting
2. Victor Crane, art dealer
3. Margaret Doyle, housekeeper
4. Dr. Julian Hale, family friend and physician
5. Sophie Laurent, Eleanor's niece and an art student

## Your task

Read the statements, the door log, and the clues. Exactly one suspect's story does not match the log. Who stole the painting?
