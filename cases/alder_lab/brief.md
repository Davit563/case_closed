# The Kestrel Chip

On Friday evening, a handful of staff stayed late at Alder Biotech's research lab.

**Kestrel**, a prototype sensor chip worth millions in research, is kept in a sealed anti-static carrier in the Clean Room. Dr. Priya Nair checked it at **17:58**. At **19:38** she found the carrier resealed and empty.

## Door system

Every internal door in the lab opens with a personal access badge. Each swipe is logged as an **entry** into that room. See `door_log.csv`.

## Rooms

Clean Room, Office Wing, Server Room, Break Room, Loading Dock, Lobby

## Suspects

1. Dr. Priya Nair, lead engineer on Kestrel
2. Tom Becker, lab technician
3. Grace Okafor, night security guard
4. Leo Marsh, engineering intern
5. Hannah Voss, finance manager

## Your task

Read the statements, the door log, and the clues. Exactly one suspect's story does not match the log. Who stole the chip?
