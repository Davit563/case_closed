# The Polar Star

On Tuesday night, the Northern Star Express sleeper train ran north toward Oslo.

**The Polar Star**, a diamond brooch belonging to Mrs. Ingrid Sorensen, was in a locked jewelry case in her sleeping compartment, Compartment A. She left it there at **21:58**. At **23:29** she returned and found the case open and empty.

## Door system

Every compartment and car door on the train opens with a keycard. Each swipe is logged as an **entry** into that compartment or car. See `door_log.csv`.

## Rooms

Compartment A, Compartment B, Compartment C, Compartment D, Dining Car, Lounge Car, Baggage Car

## Suspects

1. Arthur Pell, the conductor
2. Celia Marsh, Mrs. Sorensen's personal assistant
3. Raymond Holt, a traveling jeweler
4. Fiona Adair, a journalist
5. Nikolai Brandt, Mrs. Sorensen's nephew

## Your task

Read the statements, the door log, and the clues. Exactly one suspect's story does not match the log. Who stole the brooch?
