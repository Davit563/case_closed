# The Copperfield Takings

On Saturday night, the Copperfield Theatre was sold out for the last show of the season.

The evening's box office takings, about £8,000 in cash, were locked in the safe in the Manager's Office. Felix Grant locked them away at **20:14**. At **22:04** he opened the safe and found it empty.

## Door system

Every backstage door at the theatre opens with a staff keycard. Each swipe is logged as an **entry** into that room. See `door_log.csv`.

## Rooms

Manager's Office, Box Office, Green Room, Dressing Room, Prop Store, Stage Door

## Suspects

1. Felix Grant, the theatre manager
2. Bella Quinn, the box office cashier
3. Max Turner, a stagehand
4. Nora Fields, an understudy
5. Simon Reyes, the stage door keeper

## Your task

Read the statements, the door log, and the clues. Exactly one suspect's story does not match the log. Who stole the takings?
